The Gardens at Versaille

September 29, 2023 | Tags: Place, Image

We left early in the morning for Versailles and spent most of the day touring the palace and gardens. The palace was impressive (7/10), but the gardens were easily the highlight (10/10). We ate lunch at one of our friends' (David and Sherin) favorite spots at the back of the gardens.
